# P5: small-message figures, first pass (2026-11-14)

`dannan-bench calls` (feature/bench-small): calls offered open loop at a fixed rate,
each carrying a batch of envelopes, on the CONTROL lane, 8 s a point; `serve` as the far
end. Built Release on node-b; the far end on node-c. Raw lines:
`build/p5-sweep.jsonl` (not committed).

## The link

Both Pis are on Wi-Fi (`wlan0`): ping between them is 8 to 37 ms with heavy jitter. A
two-Pi figure measures the radio, not Dannan, so the call path's own figures are on
loopback (both nodes in one process on one Pi, sharing its four cores).

## Loopback, one Pi 5 (Dannan's call path)

| offered calls/s | answered/s | p50 ms | p99 ms | p99.9 ms | refused |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2,000 | 2,000 | 0.18 | 0.33 | 0.36 | 0 |
| 6,000 | 5,999 | 0.38 | 0.63 | 0.71 | 0 |
| 12,000 | 11,997 | 0.56 | 0.94 | 1.05 | 0 |
| 16,000 | 14,846 | 34.0 | 35.3 | 35.7 | 9,217 at `max_pending_replies` |
| 24,000 | 14,677 | 34.5 | 35.9 | 36.6 | 74,550 at `max_pending_replies` |

- **The ceiling is about 14,800 calls/s** on one Pi 5 with both ends on it. That is
  CPU, not the cap: past it, 512 calls wait and latency is 512 / 14,800 s = 35 ms
  (Little's law), and what is offered beyond is refused at once by reason. The cap
  bounds latency at saturation; it does not set the throughput. The 6,000 calls/s of
  the plan was Dagda's bench, whose handler does more; Dannan's path is not it.
- **Batching scales messages, not cost:** at 2,000 calls/s, 64 envelopes a call is
  128,000 messages/s at p99 0.39 ms (64 B) or 1.0 ms (1 KiB). Lir's 10,000 publishes/s
  is 157 calls/s of 64, or 1,250 of 8.
- **io threads:** at 12,000 calls/s, one thread saturates (refusals, 43 ms); two give
  p99 0.85 ms, four 0.94 ms, eight 1.38 ms (oversubscribed: two nodes on four cores).
  Two is enough at this load, and Macha's two stands.

The radio carries about 0.5 MB/s and a few hundred calls a second; past that, latency is
the queue and the refusals say so by reason. Nothing here is Dannan's to fix.

## Readings for the plan

- `max_pending_replies` = 512: keep it for 0.8.0. It is not what limits throughput. It
  is what bounds the wait at saturation, and refusing past it is the right answer to an
  open-loop overload. Making it per class waits for a figure that needs it.
- Lir's target, PUBACK p99 under 50 ms at QoS 1 with k = 2: Dannan's share, one
  replication round, is p99 under 1 ms on loopback up to 12,000 calls/s. On this Wi-Fi it
  is not met at any useful rate, and no Dannan change would meet it.
- Not yet measured: notify fan-out to 1, 2 and 4 subscribers; and two machines on a wired
  link, which is the figure the plan asked for.
